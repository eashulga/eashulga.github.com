I am an experienced researcher with a vast background in detector development, data analysis (ATLAS, sPHENIX, STAR), and physics-based simulations.

In my work, I adhere to the principle of adaptive planning and strive to learn and develop new skills. I am curious, self-motivated, and adaptive to a new environment.

Currently, I am an Assistant Physicist at Brookhaven National Laboratory, working on STAR data analysis, EIC/ePIC detector development and commissioning.

SCIENTIFIC INTERESTS

Detector physics

Detector development, construction of scientific equipment (tracking and particle identification) for particle physics and nuclear experiments, detector performance optimization, in-situ detector performance control systems, detector aging.

Particle physics

Physics of high-energy interactions, global event characterization, analysis of experimental data, particle tracking and particle identification at high particle density and high luminosity.

Software development

Software for fast data reconstruction (tracking, identification) and detector calibration involving AI/ML techniques.
